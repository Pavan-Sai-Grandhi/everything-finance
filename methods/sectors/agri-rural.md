## A theme that spans sectors

"Rural" is a theme, not one sector. Read it top-down, then go company by company
[soic-sectors:MWQNACOuo9I@00:05]:

1. **Where rural income comes from.** Wages, crop income and non-farm business, each with a
   sourced share [soic-sectors:MWQNACOuo9I@00:47].
2. **A stress gauge.** Demand for work under the rural employment guarantee scheme against prior
   years. Cite the Ministry of Rural Development's MIS [soic-sectors:MWQNACOuo9I@01:10].
3. **Policy.** Direct cash support (PM-KISAN) lifts income and credit capacity, while a loan
   waiver does not. Cite the scheme from the ministry [soic-sectors:MWQNACOuo9I@06:05].
4. **The dependent sectors**, each with its rural share of sales or lending, from company
   disclosures [soic-sectors:MWQNACOuo9I@01:49]:
   - two-wheelers;
   - FMCG;
   - tractors and farm equipment;
   - domestic agrochemical formulators;
   - microfinance;
   - small-ticket business loans;
   - affordable housing finance.

The theme is live when the linked sectors are cheap and their RS is rising together
[soic-sectors:MWQNACOuo9I@00:17]. Read that RS on an equal-weight basket of the names you chose,
and name them [soic-sectors:MWQNACOuo9I@26:02].

## Swing factors to check

- **Monsoon and sowing.** Use IMD rainfall against the long-period average and the agriculture
  ministry's sowing data [soic-sectors:MWQNACOuo9I@10:48].
- **Domestic against export exposure.** A domestic formulator gains from a rural recovery. An
  exporter answers to global prices and Chinese supply instead
  [soic-sectors:MWQNACOuo9I@12:36].
- **Lenders.** Farm income drives both loan demand and repayment
  [soic-sectors:MWQNACOuo9I@16:50].

## What breaks the story

- A weak monsoon.
- Companies that serve a cyclical customer missing guidance [soic-sectors:MWQNACOuo9I@14:30].
