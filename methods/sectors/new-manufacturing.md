# New-age manufacturing: electronics, semiconductors, EV, data centres, aerospace, space

**Sources:** `soic-sectors`, `plugin`. Loaded with `framework.md`. Defence has its own file, `defence.md`.

These themes share one story. Capital formation and localisation follow government support, so a domestic ecosystem forms where most value was imported before [soic-sectors:ICz5ejNhJFc@03:54] [soic-sectors:ICz5ejNhJFc@04:04]. The common method for each theme:

- Map the layers.
- Find which layer is risky and which layer has a moat.
- Size the import share that localisation can capture.
- Expect bull and bear phases inside a secular trend [soic-sectors:ICz5ejNhJFc@07:23].

## Semiconductors in more detail

- **Business models** [soic-sectors:aSBuzr4ufx0@16:49] [soic-sectors:aSBuzr4ufx0@21:26]:
  - Fabless firms only design.
  - Foundries only manufacture, like a pharma CDMO.
  - IDMs do both. An IDM that also runs a foundry competes with its own customers.
- **The chain.** Design, front-end fab, back-end assembly and test (OSAT), EMS integration
  [soic-sectors:aSBuzr4ufx0@26:02]. OSAT is labour-intensive and is where India starts.
  Mature nodes (around 28 nm) serve autos and appliances [soic-sectors:aSBuzr4ufx0@11:31].
- **Choke points.** A layer with one or two suppliers holds the power: EDA software, EUV
  lithography, leading-edge foundries [soic-sectors:aSBuzr4ufx0@31:19].
- **Fab economics.** A fab costs about $10 to 12 billion and takes close to three years to first
  wafers. It needs hundreds of chemicals and industrial gases, which favours a chemical cluster
  [soic-sectors:aSBuzr4ufx0@1:00:11] [soic-sectors:aSBuzr4ufx0@1:01:45].
- **Beneficiaries.**
  - Direct: gases, OSAT, design, capital goods.
  - Indirect: stainless tubes, transformers, chemicals.
  - Downstream: EMS, PCBs.

  For each listed name, check what share of revenue the theme actually is
  [soic-sectors:aSBuzr4ufx0@1:05:44] [soic-sectors:aSBuzr4ufx0@1:10:08].

## Swing factors to check

- **Incentives.** Scheme support as a share of capex or of sales (semiconductor mission, ECMS, PLI), cited from the ministry's notification [soic-sectors:ICz5ejNhJFc@19:13].
- **Approved projects and capex commitments** against the outlay.
- **Import share of the product** today and the target. Use the ministry or industry body.

## What breaks the story

- Cash-flow problems at a company inside a good theme are reason enough to exclude it [soic-sectors:ICz5ejNhJFc@35:50].
- The theme's end market turning down abroad (for example the US and European auto cycle for EV suppliers) [soic-sectors:ICz5ejNhJFc@32:15].
- Withdrawal of the incentive.
