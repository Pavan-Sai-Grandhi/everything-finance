## The demand story

Solar fades in the late afternoon. Wind is intermittent. Evening and night demand is rising with
air conditioning and data centres, and those need round-the-clock base load
[soic-sectors:Funuk-of7t8@02:00] [soic-sectors:Funuk-of7t8@03:01]. Cite demand and capacity
figures from the CEA.

## The last cycle, and what is different now

The 2005 to 2012 thermal build-out went bust through a chain of causes
[soic-sectors:Funuk-of7t8@04:33] [soic-sectors:Funuk-of7t8@06:04]:

- Imported coal re-priced.
- Domestic coal fell short.
- Approvals were delayed.
- Demand grew slower than capacity, so the plant load factor collapsed.
- Leverage was high, and over twenty plants went to insolvency.

Before calling a new cycle, check which of those causes is absent now. Long PPAs in place of
merchant (spot) sales are the main one to check [soic-sectors:Funuk-of7t8@09:00].

Stranded assets bought through insolvency cost a fraction of greenfield per MW and start within
months. They earn far higher equity returns while the cycle turns
[soic-sectors:Funuk-of7t8@09:57].

## Value chain

The layers are [soic-sectors:Funuk-of7t8@17:05]:

- Fuel.
- Boiler, turbine and generator (BTG), the technology core [soic-sectors:Funuk-of7t8@15:34].
- High-pressure piping and boiler tubes.
- EPC and balance of plant.
- Generation.
- Emission control.

Transmission is a separate business.

## KPIs

- **Plant load factor**, from the CEA [soic-sectors:Funuk-of7t8@06:39].
- **Share of capacity under long PPAs** against merchant sales.
- **Equipment order books** for the BTG, piping and EPC layers.

## Judging the suppliers

Score each company on growth against its barrier to entry [soic-sectors:Funuk-of7t8@1:05:23]. A
certified, high-temperature alloy line, such as P91 steam piping or IBR-certified boiler tubes,
has few qualified makers and holds pricing power [soic-sectors:Funuk-of7t8@1:00:11].

## What breaks the story

- The chain of causes from the last cycle coming back: fuel, approvals, slow demand, leverage.
- A valuation that prices the order book before it is executed.
