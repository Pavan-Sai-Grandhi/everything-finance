## The story

Activity is moving from physical to digital, on government-built rails such as UPI, Aadhaar
e-KYC, DigiLocker and ONDC. That makes the niches grow fast, and many have one or two dominant
players [soic-sectors:8yL5g4Gd8DY@00:52] [soic-sectors:8yL5g4Gd8DY@24:12]. Cite volumes from
NPCI, UIDAI or the ministry.

For each sub-segment, ask four things [soic-sectors:8yL5g4Gd8DY@05:22]:

- How big is it?
- How many players are there?
- Which way is profitability trending?
- Who controls the profit pool?

## What breaks the story

- One product carrying the profit pool, such as retail F&O for exchanges and brokers, meeting a
  regulator's change. Cite the SEBI circular.
- Reported profit that ignores ESOP cost. Adjust for it before reading the multiple
  [soic-sectors:8yL5g4Gd8DY@14:47].
