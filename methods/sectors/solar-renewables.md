## Battery energy storage (BESS)

**The need.** Solar output peaks during the day, while demand peaks in the evening and night.
Wind is intermittent. Storage covers the gap and stabilises the grid
[soic-sectors:BQAPVmZC72o@01:08].

**The chain.** It runs from minerals and processing, through cell materials, cells, modules and
racks, to containers with inverters, transformers and controls, then developers and EPC. Cells
hold about 50 to 70% of the capex. Most Indian makers import cells today and assemble the rest
[soic-sectors:BQAPVmZC72o@09:32] [soic-sectors:BQAPVmZC72o@12:03].

**How a young ecosystem forms.** Assembly comes first, on imported cells. Policy then pulls cell
manufacturing onshore through a PLI scheme, an approved-manufacturer list or a duty
[soic-sectors:BQAPVmZC72o@15:41]. Until that protection exists, Chinese cost leadership makes
local cell and chemical capex the riskiest layer [soic-sectors:BQAPVmZC72o@45:02].

**Who earns first.** Developers who locked a tariff gain when battery prices fall, then EPC firms,
then manufacturers. Assembly is commoditised and earns thin margins
[soic-sectors:BQAPVmZC72o@50:47] [soic-sectors:BQAPVmZC72o@47:13].
