## How to read it

This is a top-down sector. The most important skill is knowing where each commodity sits in its cycle [soic-sectors:osKVi2JSIzQ@04:15]. High prices pull in new mines and capacity, supply overshoots as demand slows, and prices crash [soic-sectors:osKVi2JSIzQ@04:50].

Judge a company on four parameters [soic-sectors:osKVi2JSIzQ@16:13]:

1. **Demand outlook** from the user industries.
2. **Production capacity**: how rich the reserves are and how fast new mines can start.
3. **Selling price.** Supply and demand set it, and the cost of the largest producer sets the floor.
4. **Efficiency gains.**

Demand and price are global and outside any one company's control. Weigh them about equally with the company's own execution [soic-sectors:osKVi2JSIzQ@17:56].

## Value chain

- **Upstream** (miners and explorers) is the pure play on the commodity.
- **Downstream** (refiners, smelters, wire and product makers) is a spread business [soic-sectors:osKVi2JSIzQ@1:13:08].
- **Quality suppliers** of equipment and services to the chain are the alternative to owning a miner [soic-sectors:osKVi2JSIzQ@1:15:29].
- **Raw-material self-sufficiency.** Within one metal, prefer the producer with surplus captive ore or energy [soic-sectors:osKVi2JSIzQ@1:18:31].

## KPIs

- **EBITDA per tonne**, and the domestic premium over import parity [varsity-sa].
- **Cost position.** Where the producer sits on the global cost curve.
- **Volume growth** against guidance.
- **Reserve life and the reserve replacement ratio** for oil and gas, and the time from discovery to production [soic-sectors:osKVi2JSIzQ@39:15].
- **Replacement value of the assets against enterprise value** [soic-sectors:osKVi2JSIzQ@57:05].
- **Debt to equity.** Cyclicality plus leverage is the danger [varsity-sa].

## Swing factors to check

- **LME prices and Chinese spreads.** Treat copper as a lead indicator of industrial activity [varsity-sa] [soic-sectors:osKVi2JSIzQ@06:02].
- **China exposure.** Steel and iron ore carry the most, coal and oil the least. A producer whose output is consumed domestically is shielded [soic-sectors:osKVi2JSIzQ@1:16:17].
- **Government.** Land allotment, environment and forest clearances, royalty and export bans decide supply growth in India [soic-sectors:osKVi2JSIzQ@1:20:05]. Cite the order.
- **New-supply lead time.** A new copper mine takes about two decades, so a tight market stays tight [soic-sectors:osKVi2JSIzQ@54:56].

## Risk by customer

- A producer selling on long contracts to utilities is lower risk.
- One selling into a spot, import-linked local market is higher risk and higher return [soic-sectors:osKVi2JSIzQ@32:55].
- A supplier with most revenue abroad has its growth capped by global capex [soic-sectors:osKVi2JSIzQ@1:00:03].
