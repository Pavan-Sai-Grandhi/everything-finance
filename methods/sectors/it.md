## Sub-groups

The sector is heterogeneous: company size, client industries and growth stage differ, so each group has a different problem [soic-sectors:PM4Tu3Oj-YU@02:50].

| Group | Economics | Typical problem |
|---|---|---|
| Large-cap services | Diversified legacy firms that reorient with each technology wave. High payout, slow growth [soic-sectors:PM4Tu3Oj-YU@03:37] | Growth too slow for the multiple |
| Mid-cap services | Smaller and faster-growing, with lower margins [soic-sectors:PM4Tu3Oj-YU@04:25] | The multiple already prices the growth |
| Engineering R&D (ER&D) | R&D outsourcing for auto, aerospace and industrial clients [soic-sectors:PM4Tu3Oj-YU@05:19] | Margins swing with the client industry's cycle |
| Products and SaaS | Licence or subscription revenue [soic-sectors:PM4Tu3Oj-YU@06:21] | Margins dip whenever the company reinvests profit in product or sales |

## KPIs

- **Constant-currency revenue growth and the deal TCV trend** [varsity-sa].
- **EBIT margin** against the company's own band. Falling attrition is a margin tailwind [varsity-sa].
- **Utilisation and headcount additions** are leading indicators [varsity-sa]. ER&D firms keep a bigger bench of project-trained engineers. Their fixed wage cost means margins rise fast with growth and fall fast when growth stalls [soic-sectors:PM4Tu3Oj-YU@18:35].
- **Products.** Read margin and sales growth together. The first quarter in which operating leverage shows can mark an earnings breakout [soic-sectors:PM4Tu3Oj-YU@25:13] [soic-sectors:PM4Tu3Oj-YU@30:11]. Capitalised development spend flatters reported profit, so read the cash flow [soic-sectors:PM4Tu3Oj-YU@26:51].

## Swing factors to check

- **USD-INR** moves the whole sector [varsity-sa].
- **The client industry's cycle.** ER&D follows auto and manufacturing R&D budgets, particularly in Europe [soic-sectors:PM4Tu3Oj-YU@21:29].
- **US discretionary and government tech spending**, read from the managements' guidance [soic-sectors:PM4Tu3Oj-YU@12:47].
- **Technology disruption** (AI agents today, earlier Y2K, SaaS and cloud). Hear the bear case (fewer billable hours, the per-head billing model hit first) and the bull case (each wave created new deployment work). Then track revenue and margin for one to two years rather than assume either [soic-sectors:PM4Tu3Oj-YU@31:09] [soic-sectors:PM4Tu3Oj-YU@33:42].

## What breaks the story

- A multiple far above the band from before the last cycle while growth is mature [soic-sectors:PM4Tu3Oj-YU@00:13].
- Managements starting to talk about cost cutting or optimisation [soic-sectors:PM4Tu3Oj-YU@23:39].
- Guidance missed repeatedly. Treat the name as outside the circle of competence [soic-sectors:PM4Tu3Oj-YU@28:39].
