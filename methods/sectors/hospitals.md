**Sources:** `soic-sectors`, `plugin`. Loaded with `framework.md`. Drug makers are in `pharma.md`.

## The story

India is under-bedded, and listed private hospitals hold a small share of beds against the public
sector. Volume growth has run in double digits for years outside the Covid year. Cite bed
counts from the health ministry or the National Health Profile
[soic-sectors:lKtSrARFu6Q@01:10] [soic-sectors:lKtSrARFu6Q@01:25].

## Sub-groups

| Group | Character |
|---|---|
| Pan-India multi-specialty | A national brand that still dominates only in some states [soic-sectors:lKtSrARFu6Q@03:17] |
| Regional or one-state | Can be very profitable on a strong local brand [soic-sectors:lKtSrARFu6Q@07:22] |
| Single specialty (child care, cancer, joints) | Child care is seasonal, peaking in the flu quarters [soic-sectors:lKtSrARFu6Q@02:12] [soic-sectors:lKtSrARFu6Q@13:35] |
| Overseas units | Different margins; read them separately [soic-sectors:lKtSrARFu6Q@06:18] |

A hospital brand is local. Patients and doctors follow it, and it takes years to build, so a
strong brand in one city does not travel [soic-sectors:lKtSrARFu6Q@03:51].

## KPIs

- **Average revenue per occupied bed, occupancy and margin** [soic-sectors:lKtSrARFu6Q@26:46].
- **Capex by type.** Breakeven takes [soic-sectors:lKtSrARFu6Q@04:37]:
  - months for brownfield beds;
  - two to three years for a new hospital in the same city;
  - about seven years in a new city.

  The capex plan, and where it goes, sets the ROCE path [soic-sectors:lKtSrARFu6Q@07:16]
  [soic-sectors:lKtSrARFu6Q@08:11].
- **Pre-opening costs.** Doctors are hired before revenue arrives, so a new unit dilutes margin
  first [soic-sectors:lKtSrARFu6Q@14:57].
- **Government business share and cash flow**, where government schemes pay slowly
  [soic-sectors:lKtSrARFu6Q@26:20].

## Reading the index

There is no NSE hospital index. Read RS on a named basket of listed hospitals and say which names
are in it [soic-sectors:lKtSrARFu6Q@11:16].

## What breaks the story

- Today's ROCE is the payoff of a past capex cycle with low returns
  [soic-sectors:lKtSrARFu6Q@05:13]. A new expansion into unfamiliar cities resets it.
- A valuation that prices the mature ROCE before the new beds have broken even.
