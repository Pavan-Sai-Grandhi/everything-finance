# Business services: facility management and staffing

**Sources:** `soic-sectors`, `plugin`. Loaded with `framework.md`.

## The story

Companies are moving non-core building and factory work from in-house teams to specialists.
That migration drives the industry's growth more than the economy does. Track the in-house share
over time from the industry reports that the annual-report MD&A cites
[soic-sectors:AirPj2owHLA@02:25].

## KPIs

- **Margin.** Mid-single-digit EBITDA margins are normal. ROE comes from asset-light turnover,
  not margin [soic-sectors:AirPj2owHLA@05:10].
- **Mix.** Track the share of higher-margin services, and the exit of low-margin contracts
  [soic-sectors:AirPj2owHLA@19:50].
- **Customer type.** Government contracts scale easily but collect slowly, so read receivables
  days beside revenue [soic-sectors:AirPj2owHLA@41:21].

## What breaks the story

- Manpower dependence, strikes and unionisation [soic-sectors:AirPj2owHLA@39:10].
- Margins that stay flat as the company grows. Few players achieve margin expansion.
- For a group being demerged, value each part on its own multiple and add them up, and allow for
  delay [soic-sectors:AirPj2owHLA@34:24] [soic-sectors:AirPj2owHLA@36:31].
