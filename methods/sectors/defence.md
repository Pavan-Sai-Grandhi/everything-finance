# Defence

chains are in `new-manufacturing.md`.

## The story

Demand rises with the number of regional conflicts and with the neighbourhood India sits in.
Defence spending is an arms race, since a country spends to stay level with its rival
[soic-sectors:yfcouNoKV3w@11:46] [soic-sectors:yfcouNoKV3w@20:00]. Import substitution decides who
gets the orders, through import-ban lists and indigenous-content categories under the Defence
Acquisition Procedure. Cite the Ministry of Defence document for each list and threshold
[soic-sectors:yfcouNoKV3w@28:06].

Warfare is shifting from expensive platforms to cheap drones, electronics, air defence and
electronic warfare. That changes which suppliers grow [soic-sectors:yfcouNoKV3w@05:44]
[soic-sectors:yfcouNoKV3w@34:42].

## Value chain

| Tier | Who | Character |
|---|---|---|
| End user | Army, Navy, Air Force, export buyers | One customer, who sets price and timing |
| Tier 1 platforms | DPSUs (aircraft, missiles, ships) | Long order cycles tied to one platform [soic-sectors:yfcouNoKV3w@44:17] |
| Systems and assemblies | Electronics, simulators, forgings | A product goes into any platform, so the order flow is steadier [soic-sectors:yfcouNoKV3w@1:13:31] |
| Sub-systems and components | RF, optics, precision parts | Build-to-spec firms design the product and have higher barriers than build-to-print contract makers [soic-sectors:yfcouNoKV3w@1:01:44] |

## KPIs

For each company, check three things [soic-sectors:yfcouNoKV3w@43:18]:

- **Products.**
- **Order book**, including order book to sales, which gives the years of revenue it covers.
- **The addressable market** for those products.

Also check:

- **Working capital.** The government is the buyer and receivables stretch, so read the cash
  conversion along with the profit [soic-sectors:yfcouNoKV3w@32:49].
- **Replacement cycle.** Ships last 30 to 40 years, while electronics need new products every
  year [soic-sectors:yfcouNoKV3w@32:28].
- **Export orders** against the government's export target, cited from the ministry
  [soic-sectors:yfcouNoKV3w@32:27].

## Swing factors to check

- The defence budget's capital outlay, from the Union Budget documents.
- New indigenisation lists and DAP amendments, from the Ministry of Defence.
- Order inflow against the order book. A delay can be one-off or a trend; read the concall
  [soic-sectors:yfcouNoKV3w@52:13].

## Reading the index

The Nifty Defence index is top-heavy: two or three names are 40 to 50% of its weight. Read RS on
a named basket split by sub-group as well as on the index, and say which basket it is
[soic-sectors:yfcouNoKV3w@1:16:49].

## What breaks the story

- Valuations that already price years of order execution. A structural theme still has lulls
  and corrections [soic-sectors:yfcouNoKV3w@1:20:09].
- Order slippage or a platform programme being delayed.
- Receivables building up faster than revenue.
