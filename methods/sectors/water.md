## The story

India's share of the world's fresh water is far smaller than its share of the world's
people, and city groundwater is running down. Government schemes such as the Jal Jeevan Mission fund the build-out
[soic-sectors:-fp3XootfB4@01:31] [soic-sectors:-fp3XootfB4@02:18]. Cite water availability from
the Central Water Commission or NITI Aayog, and scheme outlays from the Ministry of Jal Shakti.

Study it in this order [soic-sectors:-fp3XootfB4@03:37]:

1. The schemes.
2. The value chain.
3. Each sub-sector.
4. The conclusion.

## Value chain

The layers are [soic-sectors:-fp3XootfB4@08:15]:

- EPC contractors (drinking water, sewage treatment, desalination, irrigation).
- Wastewater treatment.
- Water chemicals.
- Capital goods (valves, pumps).
- Ductile iron pipes.

| Model | Economics |
|---|---|
| Pure EPC | Build and hand over. Margins near 10%; retention money stretches working capital [soic-sectors:-fp3XootfB4@21:55] [soic-sectors:-fp3XootfB4@28:11] |
| Hybrid annuity or BOT | The asset sits on the balance sheet and earns an annuity [soic-sectors:-fp3XootfB4@22:40] |
| Operation and maintenance | Services after the build, with better margins and returns than EPC [soic-sectors:-fp3XootfB4@23:24] |
| Capital goods | Customised equipment holds pricing better than standard goods [soic-sectors:-fp3XootfB4@43:16] |

## KPIs

- Order book, the type of orders, and new order intake [soic-sectors:-fp3XootfB4@09:35].
- Working capital and receivables [soic-sectors:-fp3XootfB4@34:36].
- Margin guidance against delivery [soic-sectors:-fp3XootfB4@25:48].
- For pipe makers, EBITDA per kilogram through the cycle [soic-sectors:-fp3XootfB4@14:37].

## What breaks the story

- The build-out ends. Once pipes are laid the spend does not repeat, so two or three good years
  are followed by several bad ones [soic-sectors:-fp3XootfB4@15:17].
- Peak margins taken as normal. A tender-driven sector needs an exit plan decided in advance
  [soic-sectors:-fp3XootfB4@20:42].
