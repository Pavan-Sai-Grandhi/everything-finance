## Value chain

Two companies both called "pharma" can have entirely different economics. Place a company on this chain before judging it [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@03:12].

| Layer | Economics |
|---|---|
| Starting materials and intermediates | B2B chemistry. Niche intermediates (for example contrast media) can be consolidated markets with 3 to 4 global suppliers [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@06:15] |
| Generic API | A commodity: margins cycle around a normal band [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@04:59] [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@54:38] |
| Speciality API | Niche, high-priced molecules [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@05:56] |
| Domestic branded formulations | Like FMCG: the brand earns a premium [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@04:04] |
| Export generics | US price erosion. First-to-file (Para IV) gives 18 to 30 months of exclusive sale [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@08:14] |
| Trade generics | Unbranded, sold through distributors, without medical reps [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@34:01] |
| CDMO / CRAMS / CRDMO | Contract work for innovators. Gestation is 3 to 5 years and contracts are sticky [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@09:16] [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@29:15] |
| NCE innovators | R&D on new molecules, with binary outcomes [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@10:37] |

## KPIs

- **Product mix.** Track the share of revenue from the high-margin layers (CDMO, speciality API, branded) [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@16:53]. Read segment margins separately, because a blended margin hides a strong or a weak segment [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@45:30].
- **Capex productivity.** Compare incremental fixed-asset turn and incremental sales with the capex spent [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@31:43]. An asset turn near 1x caps ROCE even while profit grows [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@49:00]. Idle formulation capacity creates negative operating leverage [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@29:42].
- **US generics.** Price erosion and the ANDA pipeline [plugin].
- **India.** Chronic versus acute mix [plugin].

## Judging a CDMO

The SCALE UP checklist [soic-sectors:sdG9b3Xue-k@22:25]:

- **Stickiness.** The share of revenue from commercial-stage molecules against development work,
  which can fail in trials. A supplier named in the regulatory filing is hard to replace,
  because switching means re-filing in every market [soic-sectors:sdG9b3Xue-k@22:38]
  [soic-sectors:sdG9b3Xue-k@12:20].
- **Chemistry.** Capabilities the client lacks in-house (flow chemistry, HPAPI, peptides,
  oligonucleotides, ADCs), and whether that revenue share is rising
  [soic-sectors:sdG9b3Xue-k@25:47].
- **Anchor client and concentration** [soic-sectors:sdG9b3Xue-k@29:26].
- **Late-stage pipeline.** Count the late-stage and commercial molecules. A CDMO takes 10 to 15
  years to build [soic-sectors:sdG9b3Xue-k@29:58] [soic-sectors:sdG9b3Xue-k@30:41].
- **Economics of incremental capex.** A dedicated block against a signed contract beats
  multipurpose capacity. Brownfield earns more than greenfield. Plants abroad earn less, and an
  acquired asset can take five years or more to stabilise [soic-sectors:sdG9b3Xue-k@33:35]
  [soic-sectors:sdG9b3Xue-k@34:13] [soic-sectors:sdG9b3Xue-k@35:04].
- **Utilisation and runway.** Near 100% utilisation leaves no room to grow, and management talk
  of destocking is a warning [soic-sectors:sdG9b3Xue-k@36:01].
- **Price against the cycle.** A high multiple plus a destocking event is the worst combination
  [soic-sectors:sdG9b3Xue-k@40:03].

Results are lumpy. Calendar-year customers and 4 to 8 week reactor campaigns can put a year's
supply in one quarter, so judge a CDMO over two to three years [soic-sectors:sdG9b3Xue-k@36:36]
[soic-sectors:sdG9b3Xue-k@37:42]. A company that depends on one molecule carries destocking risk,
and innovators give only 6 to 12 months of visibility [soic-sectors:sdG9b3Xue-k@39:16]. A
second-source supplier only adds capacity and is the first to lose orders
[soic-sectors:sdG9b3Xue-k@49:46].

## Swing factors to check

- **Molecule approvals and customer destocking** for CDMOs, read from concalls and regulator decisions.
- **Policy on competing supply chains** (for example US restrictions on Chinese CDMOs), and China moving from supplier to competitor of its own clients [soic-sectors:sdG9b3Xue-k@08:21]. Judge it by the order book, not the headline [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@26:02].

## What breaks the story

- Loan covenant breaches, a sign of weak financial management [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@38:10].
- A concentration risk: one molecule or one customer going generic or leaving.
- Barriers that do not show up in margins. Radiopharma has high barriers and still thin margins [soic-sectors:1hKTKUTxtLw@45:10].
